
# 🍜 KYODO – Asian Kitchen & Tea Room

### A Taste of Asia, A Moment of Togetherness.

KYODO is a modern restaurant website designed for an Asian Kitchen & Tea Room. It aims to provide visitors with an engaging digital experience to explore Asian cuisine, discover food offerings, and learn about the restaurant.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://kyodo-kitchen.vercel.app/

## ✨ Features

- **Modern Homepage:** Attractive restaurant landing page.
- **Asian Food Experience:** Showcase of Asian cuisine and food offerings.
- **Tea Room Concept:** Highlighting the relaxing tea experience.
- **Food Presentation:** Visually appealing presentation of restaurant dishes.
- **Restaurant Information:** Information about the restaurant and its identity.
- **User-Friendly Navigation:** Simple and intuitive browsing experience.
- **Responsive Design:** Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Modern UI/UX:** Clean layout with an engaging restaurant-focused design.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of KYODO is to build a modern online presence for an Asian Kitchen & Tea Room.

The website aims to connect customers with the restaurant through an attractive interface, making it easier to explore its food experience and restaurant information.
